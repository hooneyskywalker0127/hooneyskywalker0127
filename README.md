### 🔬 Research focus
- **Humanoid whole-body control** — motion retargeting, tracking, and skill composition on Unitree G1 / IGRIS-C
- **Vision-Language-Action models** — GR00T, Cosmos Policy, action tokenizers and what happens when they're wrong
- **World models for manipulation** — calibration, long-horizon benchmarks (CALVIN, LIBERO)
- **Humanoid swarms** — Isaac Sim digital twins of heterogeneous factory cells
